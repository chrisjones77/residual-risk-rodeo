## Short description

A safety committee discovered blast beats and a banjo. Twelve songs later, the hazards remain, the controls have started harmonizing, and the river is still running.

## Long description

`Residual Risk Rodeo` follows a fictional Northern Ontario safety committee as it tries to document, control, and eventually understand the music gathering around the Wasi River. Bluegrass, gospel, worksite folk, industrial percussion, and blast beats carry stories about near misses, permits, fatigue, design, responsibility, and collective care. What begins as procedural satire becomes a songbook about the limits of paperwork and the practical value of listening to one another.
