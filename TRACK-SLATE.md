# Residual Risk Rodeo: Production Slate

## Album arc

The committee begins convinced that every problem can be controlled with a form. The controls become progressively stranger as bluegrass, metal, gospel, and worksite noise enter the process. By the final act, the committee realizes that safety is not the elimination of uncertainty but the practice of caring for people while the river keeps moving.

## Sequence

1. **Residual Risk Rodeo** - The committee convenes and discovers that everything technically controlled is still alive.
2. **The Banjo Is Not the Hazard** - The first argument: blame the instrument or examine the system?
3. **Corrective Action Boogie** - Failed fixes become dance instructions.
4. **Near Miss at the Gravel Pit** - The comedy gives way to a concrete story with real stakes.
5. **Blast Beat Evacuation Drill** - The album's first total genre collision.
6. **Hierarchy of Controls** - A sincere centrepiece about changing conditions at the source.
7. **Lockout / Tagout / Let It Roll** - Energy returns as workshop bluegrass-metal.
8. **The Observer Signs the Permit** - Documentation without intervention becomes its own hazard.
9. **PPE for the Soul** - A gospel interlude about the limits of personal protection.
10. **Incident Investigation at Mile 46** - Conflicting testimony around a bridge, a storm, and an old machine.
11. **Banjo as Control** - The committee finally understands the music as a form of collective care.
12. **Closeout Meeting / River Still Running** - Forms are filed; uncertainty and community remain.

## Workshop candidates

- **A Thorn, Not a Threat** - After the formal closeout, one unresolved memory remains. Two candidates are archived, but the song is on workshop hold and outside the canonical sequence.
- **Water Under the Bridge I Can Burn** - The narrator realizes that release and agency can coexist: the past has passed, and he can close only his own route back. Written but not generated.

## Optional outtake

**Open Flame, Open Tuning** can become a single, bonus track, or bridge into the next collection.

## Production rule

Generate at least two contrasting versions of tracks 1, 5, 6, 10, and 12 before selecting a sequence. These tracks carry the narrative architecture and should not be accepted merely because the first generation is catchy.
