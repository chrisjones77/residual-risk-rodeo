# Album Direction Prompt

Create a concept album for Wasi River Bluegrass called Residual Risk Rodeo.

A Northern Ontario safety committee discovers blast beats and a banjo. Use hazards, controls, corrective actions, permits, near misses, and incident investigations as lyrical language, while letting the songs carry emotional weight beneath the humour.

Begin with bluegrass and expand into square dance, gospel, industrial percussion, metal, drones, and worksite folk. Keep the banjo audible as a recurring signature. Use concrete places, weather, tools, bridges, gravel, machinery, and human voices.
