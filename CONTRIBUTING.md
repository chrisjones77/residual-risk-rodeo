# Contributing

Residual Risk Rodeo is an open creative process. Contributions can include:

- new prompt variations or arrangement ideas;
- corrections to metadata and documentation;
- safety, engineering, or bluegrass references that strengthen the work;
- accessible ways to explain the album's process.

Please keep contributions concrete, respectful, and easy to reproduce. Describe what changed, why it changed, and what evidence supports the change. Do not submit private personal information, credentials, or material you do not have permission to share.

For substantial creative changes, open an issue first so the project can keep a clear canon while preserving experiments.
